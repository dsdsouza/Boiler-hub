You can see everyone’s calendar. You can’t see their pressure.

Boilerplate turns each person’s calendar and tasks into a live boiler. Meetings, deadlines and homework add pressure; finished tasks and free time vent it. Teams see who is near the red line, only with their permission.

How the pressure moves

Pressure in

Connect Google Calendar and add tasks. Meetings, deadlines and homework over the next 7 days build the score from 0 to 100.

Steam out

Finished tasks, focus time and days off open the release valve. Complete a task and watch the needle drop.

Shared on your terms

Join a class or work team and choose who sees your gauge: Hidden, Lead only, or Everyone. Hidden is the default.

Four zones, one glance

The gauge measures load, not wellbeing.

Cool · 0–39
Calm, light steam
Working · 40–69
Steady steam
Hot · 70–89
Shaking, heavy steam, amber gauge
Redline · 90–100
Whistle, red glow, strong shake

We never store what is on your calendar.

Teams see a number, a zone and your status, only if you choose. Never event titles, attendees or times. You can hide from any team at any time.

HIDDEN Nobody sees your boiler. The default when you join.
LEAD ONLY Only the team’s leads or teacher see it.
EVERYONE Everyone on that team sees your gauge and status.
